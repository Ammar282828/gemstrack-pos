place in `UITests/ERPUITests.swift` opened in the demo, the sheets most used, launch and scrolling timed),
as `[ui]` in a commit did on GitHub's Mac minutes until 2026-10-09. Results show on the commit in GitHub
("Xcode Cloud" check) and in App Store Connect → Xcode Cloud. One line per check, newest last.
